Beef Stew

Description

Beef stew has nutritional value for the body - builds muscle fast and increases the muscle fibers. it really makes my dish very appetizing and more enjoyable especially with Ugali.

Ingridients

  • 500 g Beef (cow meet)
  • 1 large Onion
  • 2 tbsp Cooking oil
  • 3/4 Tomatoes
  • 1 medium Capsicum (bell pepper)
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 3 medium Potatoes
  • 3 medium Carrots
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Curry power or paprika or black pepper

Steps

  1. Cut beef into bite-sized pieces. Chop all vegetables
  2. Heat oil in a pot, fry beedf until browned
  3. Add onions and garlic, saute until soft
  4. Add tomatoes and capsium, cook until tomatoes break down
  5. Stir in carrots and potatoes
  6. Season with salt, soy sauce, and your chosen spice.
  7. Add enough water to cover ingredients. bring to boil.
  8. Lower head, cover, and simmer for about 45-60 minutes until meat and veggies are tender
  9. Serve hot with ugali or rice.

Fried Meatball Stew

Description

Meatball stew is a very easy and delicious dish to prepare at home. I always and mostly used minced meat to prepare samosas, little did I know something more delicious could be extracted from it to accompany my dishes. My favourite accompaniment is when eating the fried meatball stew with spaghetti.

Ingredients

  • 200 g Minced meat
  • 2 tbsp Coriander (Chopped)
  • 1/2 Eggs
  • 1 tbspPaprika
  • 1/2 tbsp Blakc pepper
  • 1 tsp Curry Powder
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1/2 Onion (chopped)
  • 1 clove Garlic(minced)
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 2 tbsp Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Mix minced meat, coriander, egg, paprika, black pepper, curry powder, and kfc-crispy-chicken-kenyan-style-recipe-recipe-main-photo
  2. Shape into small balls.
  3. Heat oil in a pan, add meatballs, and wet fry until browned and juicy.
  4. Add onion and garlic, saute until soft.
  5. Stir in tomato paste and soy sauce, cook for 2 min.
  6. Add splash of water if needed, simmer meatballs in sauce for 5 min.
  7. Serve over boiled spaghetti/rice.

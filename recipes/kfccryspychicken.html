KFC Crispy Chicken

Description

Golden, crunchy-crisp coating wrapping juicy, tender chicken — that unmistakable Original Recipe blend of herbs and spices. Hot, freshly fried, satisfying with every bite. Perfect for sharing with family or treating yourself. Finger-lickin' good, always!

Ingredients

  • 500 g Chicken pieces (Drumsticks, wings or thighs
  • 1 Cupcooking Oil (for frying)
  • marinade
  • HalfCup milk or buttermilk
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 cloves Garlic (crushed
  • 1 tbspGinger(grated)
  • 1 tbsp Salt
  • Half Teaspoon black Pepper
  • 1 tbsp Paprika
  • 1 tbsp Chicken Masala
  • Half tbsp Curry Powder

    • Crispy Coating

  • 1 Cup Wheat flour
  • 2 tbspCorn flour (for extra ccrispiness
  • 1 tbsp Paprika
  • 1 tbsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp Black pepper
  • 1 Half tbsp Chilli powder. (Optional)

Steps

  1. Cean the chicken pieces and make small cuts
  2. Mix Milk, Lemon juice, garlic, ginger, salt and the spices. Add the chicken and coat well.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for 1 or 2 hours.
  4. Mix flour, corn flour, paprika, garlic powder, black pepper, Chilli, and salt in a bowl
  5. Remove chicken chicken from marinade. Coat each piece in the flour mixture.
  6. For extra crunch, dip again in maridane then coat with flour again.
  7. Heat oil on medium heat
  8. Fry the chicken pieaces until golden brown and crispy. Cook until the inside is fully done. (bout 10 -15 minutes depending size)
  9. Remove and place on paper towel to drain excess oil
  10. Serve with either chips, coleslaw. kachumbari, soda or fresh juice.
